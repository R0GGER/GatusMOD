Status pages

A status page shows only the endpoints of the chosen groups and single endpoints, with its own title and heading. It is always available at /status/<slug> on this site. On its own domains it is the whole site: only its endpoints can be read there, and the admin and the badges are not available.

A status page with a password is private: visitors log in with that password, and its endpoints are hidden on this site, in its API and badges, and on the other status pages.
